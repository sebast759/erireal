Ericeira and Mafra · Homes to rent · Asking rents · October 2026

Across the whole municipality of Mafra, apartments asked €1,500 and houses €1,700. Seasonal lets and short stays

30 of the 238 homes advertised in Mafra this month say, in their own text, that they are for rent only part of the year (for example October to April) or for a short stay. Their rents cannot be compared with a long-term rent, so they are not in any median above. 129 ads say nothing about the duration and are counted as long-term, so a few seasonal lets may still sit among them.

How to read these numbers

Every figure is an asking rent from listing data, not a signed rent. A home advertised more than once is counted once. Apartments and houses are never pooled, and a median needs enough ads to mean something, so small groups are left out.

Área Útil is the actual living space; Área Bruta is the advertised gross area, which can include walls, garages, terraces and storage. €/m² Útil and €/m² Bruta are the asking price divided by each. All figures are asking prices from listing data, not transaction prices.
