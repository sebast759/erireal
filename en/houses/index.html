Ericeira house prices: asking price per m² and by bedrooms

Ericeira parish · Homes for sale · Asking prices · October 2026

In October 2026, Ericeira had 435 homes for sale, with a median asking price of €698k. The median asking price per m² of Área Útil, the actual living space, was €5,000/m². Since December 2025, when this record starts, homes for sale are down 22.0% and the €/m² Útil is up 7.9%. A median moves with the mix of homes on the market, so one month on its own is not a trend.

Listing activity

67 homes appeared that were not online in September 2026, and 81 that were online in September 2026 were gone. A listing leaving the market is not a recorded sale: it can be withdrawn, expire, or come back later under a new ID. Among the homes listed in both months, 18 cut the asking price and 1 raised it.

How to read these numbers

Every figure is an asking price from listing data for homes for sale in the Ericeira parish, read once a month. Portugal does not publish transaction prices per property, so what can be measured is what sellers want, not what buyers paid. Medians are used because a few very expensive homes would distort an average. Time is counted in months, since the data is monthly.

Área Útil is the actual living space; Área Bruta is the advertised gross area, which can include walls, garages, terraces and storage. €/m² Útil and €/m² Bruta are the asking price divided by each. All figures are asking prices from listing data, not transaction prices.
