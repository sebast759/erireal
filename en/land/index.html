How to read these numbers

Plots are sorted into the three classes the listings state: urban (urbano), urbanisable (urbanizável) and rural (rústico). Their prices are completely different, so they are never pooled into one average. When several adverts for the same plot disagree on its class, the lowest class is used, because advertisers tend to flatter a plot; 47 plots that give no class are left out of every figure. A plot advertised by several agencies is counted once.

Asking prices, not transaction prices. Price per m² is the asking price divided by the plot area, not by any built area.

The live dashboard has the map of every plot, the size and price charts, the agency counts and the changes since last month.
