Ericeira and Mafra property prices by area

Ericeira parish and the municipality of Mafra · Asking prices · October 2026

Prices differ a lot from one part of the municipality to the next. This page puts the three markets side by side for every zone with enough listings in October 2026: homes for sale (€/m² Útil), apartment rents (per m² Útil, per month) and urban land (€/m²). Among zones with at least 8 homes for sale, Centro da Vila had the highest median asking price per m² Útil (€5,714/m²) and Igreja Nova e Cheleiros the lowest (€3,011/m²).

How to read these numbers

Each figure is a median of the listings in that zone. The three markets are measured on different listings, and a zone with few listings can move a lot from one month to the next, so use the table to compare zones, not to value a particular home.

Área Útil is the actual living space; Área Bruta is the advertised gross area, which can include walls, garages, terraces and storage. €/m² Útil and €/m² Bruta are the asking price divided by each. All figures are asking prices from listing data, not transaction prices.
