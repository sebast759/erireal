Ericeira property market report — June 2026

Ericeira, Portugal · Asking prices · June 2026 snapshot

Ericeira had 498 homes for sale on Idealista in June 2026, up 3.5% on May 2026 and down 10.8% against December 2025, when this record starts. The median asking price per m² of Área Útil — the actual living space — was €4,854/m², up 0.7% on the month and up 4.7% since December 2025. Half the 498 listings asked more than €665k, half asked less.

How to read these numbers

Every figure here is an asking price taken from public Idealista listings for homes for sale in the Ericeira parish, read once a month. Portugal does not publish transaction prices per property, so asking prices are what can be measured — they show what sellers want, not what buyers paid.

Área Útil is the actual living space. Área Bruta is the advertised gross area and can include walls, garages, terraces and storage, so a price per m² computed on Área Bruta understates what living space really costs. Both are shown above.
